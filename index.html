Everything your server keeps asking for, in one bot.

Eight command categories, all installed at once — no separate bots for trivia, moderation, and AI chat.

Fun & games

Jokes, roasts, compliments, the magic 8-ball, dice, coin flips, and memes pulled from Reddit — the small commands that keep a channel alive between real conversations.

Trivia & points

Multiple-choice trivia with real buttons, a daily bonus claim, and a leaderboard your whole server competes on. Wager points in duels or send them to a friend.

AI chat & personas

Talk to Rune with a message prefix, no slash command needed. Switch its personality per-user — pirate, Shakespeare, robot, sarcastic, cheerful — without changing it for anyone else.

Voice

Keep Rune parked in a voice channel 24/7 with auto-reconnect, or hold down push-to-talk for a spoken reply — recorded, transcribed, and answered back in a synthesized voice.

Moderation

Kick, ban, timeout, and un-timeout members with a proper reason logged — no separate mod bot required for the basics.

Polls

Up to four options, live vote counts with visual bars, and the freedom to change a vote — closed only by whoever created it.

Utility

Set a reminder in plain minutes, check your own usage stats, or pull up the server info card — the small lookups people ask a bot for daily.

Top.gg voting

Vote for Rune on Top.gg every 12 hours and claim a points bonus — verified automatically against the Top.gg API.

AI chat that keeps working when a provider doesn't.

Rune's conversation is powered by Groq (fast responses) with OpenRouter as a fallback for deeper reasoning. If Groq is rate-limited, OpenRouter steps in automatically to keep Rune responding.

The result: fewer moments where Rune just stops responding, and no single point of failure for the feature people use most.
